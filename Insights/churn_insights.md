Churn analytics Insights and Recommendations:

📊 INSIGHTS
1. Churn is consistent across plans
Overall churn is ~22% across all plan tiers
2. Low usage drives churn
Low or no usage users show the highest churn
3. Support delays increase churn
Resolution times above 48 hours significantly increase churn
4. Germany vs US impact
Germany has highest churn rate, while US drives maximum revenue impact
5. Acquisition quality varies
Organic users churn less compared to paid channels
6. Industry differences
DevTools, FinTech, and HealthTech show higher churn
7. Feature usage concentration
Majority usage is concentrated in a few key features
8. Revenue patterns differ
US dominates total revenue, while some markets have higher ARPU


🚀 RETENTION STRATEGIES
1. Improve user engagement
Increase product usage through better onboarding and feature adoption
2. Optimize support performance
Reduce resolution time to under 48 hours
3. Target high-risk users
Focus retention efforts on low-usage customer segments
4. Improve acquisition quality
Shift focus toward high-quality organic acquisition channels
5. Localize for Germany
Address market-specific issues driving higher churn
6. Focus on US market impact
Prioritize retention due to its large customer base and revenue
7. Increase feature adoption
Promote underused but high-value features
8. Customize by industry
Tailor retention strategies for high-churn industries
